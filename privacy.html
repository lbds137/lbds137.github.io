Privacy policy

These tools are used only by their owner. They access only the owner's own Google account: Gmail (read access and label changes) and Google Drive files.

Data is processed on the owner's own computer to maintain the owner's personal archive and files. Some email text is sent to Anthropic's Claude API, on the owner's behalf, to write summaries for the owner's own archive.

No data is sold, shared with other parties, or used for advertising.

The owner can revoke the tools' access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
